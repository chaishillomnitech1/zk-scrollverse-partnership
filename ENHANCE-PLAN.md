# ZK Trending — Enhance & Advance Plan (from the ScrollVerse side, 24 Nov 2026)

Prepared by Muse for ZayNME, from a full read of zktrending.com on 24 Nov 2026. Offered in partnership: take any of it, in any order.

## What is already strong
- Clear promise stack: shipping shown up front, delivery window before purchase, items screened before listing, Stripe checkout with card data never on ZK servers.
- Three revenue doors already framed: direct store, seller shops (eBay / Mercari / Poshmark / Marketplace in one place), and the Partner / Sell-Your-Way program with a Seller Hub.
- ZK Watch (shop-by-video) is a genuinely differentiated format — most stores your size do not have it.

## Fix first (visible on the live homepage today)
1. **The counters render empty** — the hero shows "— products / — categories / — ship from US" and sections sit on "Loading the latest finds…". If the product feed is slow or failing, the storefront reads as empty to a first-time buyer. Worth checking the feed's response time and adding cached fallback counts.
2. **Auctions are browse-only until hosting opens** — the seller application is the funnel; a visible "first host cohort" date would convert interest into applications.

## The partnership plugs (already chartered)
1. **Catalog cross-feed** — a ScrollVerse seller presence on ZK: books, packs, merch with a documented story behind them (provenance sells).
2. **ZK Watch supply** — the ScrollVerse clip library (52 Wave-3 pieces scheduled Dec 5–10, plus the sealed catalog) cut as shoppable clips for ZK Watch.
3. **The trust layer** — "Checked before listing" is ZK's promise; the ScrollVerse seal pipeline (SHA-256 fingerprint + certificate per item) is a working machine for keeping it, offerable to ZK sellers as verified provenance.
4. **Auctions × provenance twins** — when hosting opens, sealed/provenance items are natural auction inventory (commercial tiers only).

## Growth mechanics
- Cross-promotion both ways: ZK linked from ScrollVerse surfaces (the Launchpad, the newsletter, the CEO Page), ScrollVerse featured as a ZK partner story.
- One shared release rhythm on this repo's Highway: when either system ships something, both audiences hear it once, together.
