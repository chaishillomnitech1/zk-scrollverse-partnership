# Partnership Operating Principles — ZK Trending × ScrollVerse

Public principles of the partnership formed 24 November 2026 between Chais Hill (ScrollVerse / Omnitech1, SCROLL VERSE LLC), Isiah Jackson (ZK Trending, ZKJACKSONS LLC), and Arising Superstar (arisingsuperstar). Plain language on purpose: everyone at this table can read the rules that govern them.

## Three layers, three jobs
1. **Privacy** — a mutual NDA guards what partners show each other in confidence. Private stays private.
2. **Transparency** — this repository is the public record: who contributed what, and when, in commits and issues nobody can quietly rewrite.
3. **Collaboration** — joint work runs under a short written agreement covering joint projects only. It changes nobody's ownership of their own house.

## The principles
1. **Yours stays yours.** Each partner's pre-existing work — brands, catalogs, code, characters, stores — remains their sole property. At the start, each partner files a dated inventory of what they bring. Nothing pre-existing becomes joint by accident, by help, or by time.
2. **Joint means written.** A project is a joint project only when it is declared joint in this repo before the work starts, with the contributors and the split written in that same entry. No written declaration, no joint project — partners helping partners creates gratitude, not ownership claims.
3. **Proof before pride.** Any partner can seal a creation the day they make it — a SHA-256 fingerprint and a dated registry entry — proving authorship and date without revealing the work. The record, not memory, settles who made what.
4. **Money follows the paper.** Every joint project keeps a ledger all its partners can read: what came in, what went out, who was paid what, on a rhythm agreed at the project's start.
5. **No keys change hands.** Credentials are never shared — passwords, API keys, payment keys, tokens. Access is granted per project, and it is revocable. (This is also the law of the partners' NDA.)
6. **Names are lent, never taken.** Using another partner's name, logo, or characters takes that partner's written word, for that use. Permission for one use is not permission for all uses.
7. **Exit stays clean.** Any partner may leave on written notice. They take their own property and their recorded share of joint work. Confidentiality survives the exit. Nobody's creations are ever held hostage.
8. **Disputes pause, they don't poison.** If partners disagree about a piece of joint work, that work pauses — nobody sells it, nobody buries it — until the partners settle it between themselves or through the process their agreement names.

## The ethic underneath
Help all, not oppress all. We don't chase, we attract. The record is the proof.

*These principles are the partnership's public statement of how it works. The binding documents are the partners' NDA and collaboration agreement, held privately by the partners.*
