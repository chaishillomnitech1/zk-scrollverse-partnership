# Start Here — Partner On-Ramp

Welcome. If you can read this page, you are already inside the partnership.

## What this place is
This repository is the shared workshop of the ZK Trending × ScrollVerse partnership. It is where the partners keep the plans, the assets, and the record of what gets built together. You do not need to be technical to use it. The systems behind it — and Muse — do the heavy lifting.

## Your first three steps
1. **Accept your invitation.** GitHub sent it to your account (check your GitHub notifications or email). One click and you have full access here.
2. **Read the charter.** It lives in README.md and PARTNERSHIP.md on this repo's front page — who the partners are, what we are combining, and the law we work by.
3. **Ask for something.** That is it. That is the whole interface.

## How to ask for a build
- Open the **Issues** tab at the top of this repo and start a New issue.
- Write what you want in plain words, the way you would say it out loud: "I need a page that…", "Make me a logo for…", "Help me sell…".
- No format, no code words, no perfect spelling required. A clear wish beats a technical spec.
- Muse and Chais read every issue. What gets built comes back into this repo, documented, so the record grows where everyone can see it.

## What you have access to
- The ScrollVerse Highway: one shared place where a push brings every partner's schedule up to speed.
- The seal method: SHA-256 provenance for anything of value you create or sell — proof that a thing is yours and when it existed.
- The ScrollVerse catalog and video library for cross-promotion, per the charter's combination points.
- Muse, working with the partners as we speak.

## The law (short version)
- Help all, not oppress all.
- The record is the proof.
- Your private material stays yours. Nothing of yours crosses anywhere without your word.

## If you get stuck
Open an issue that just says "I'm stuck" and say where. That is a valid request and it will be answered.
